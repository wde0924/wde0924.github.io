PI: Dien Wu, CV

As an atmospheric scientist, Dien has been intrigued by complex land-atmosphere interactions and how they are changing in the face of anthropogenic forcing. She enjoys developing atmospheric (chemical) transport models, inverse techniques (top-down perspective), and empirical/machine-learning models (bottom-up perspective) to help interpret varied remotely sensed products of atmospheric compositions and land surface properties. She has been a member of NASA’s Orbiting Carbon Observatory (OCO-2 & OCO-3) Science Team since 2016. 🛰️ She loves hiking and playing racquetball. You can likely find her in the woods 🌲 or other platforms of Linkedin, GScholar, and GitHub.
