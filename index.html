Research Theme

The overarching goal is to investigate human impacts on carbon, pollution, water, and energy fluxes using observations as key constraints! In our rapidly changing climate, our generation faces key challenges related to the carbon and water/energy cycles: mitigating anthropogenic and pyrogenic emissions while enhancing carbon sequestration and ensuring food security while coping with water scarcity. We are equally interested in the technical tools needed to tackle these questions, including atmospheric transport and chemistry modeling, inverse modeling, satellite remote sensing, and machine learning. Here are specific research questions about these climate-sensitive systems:

💧 🌾 Agricultural Land (water usage, soil emissions)

  • How much water do crop trees in semi-arid croplands consume?
  • What about NOx and N2O from cropland?

🔥 Fires (wildfires, urban fires, prescribed burns, fire-induced weather)

  • How does fire-induced weather impact pollution dispersion?
  • What about wildland–urban interface (WUI) fires?

🚗 Bridging Carbon and Air Pollutants

  • Do heavy industries burn less efficiently?
  • Can emission mitigation be detected and verified from space?

🌆 🌳 Urban Carbon (fossil fuel combustion, vegetation)

  • How much anthropogenic CO2 do global cities produce?
  • How much CO2 does urban vegetation take up, and how will land-use change alter it?
